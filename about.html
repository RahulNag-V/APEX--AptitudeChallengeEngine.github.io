HOW TO PLAY
  • Select a mode and question count to begin.
  • Answer before the timer runs out.
  • Every 2 wrong answers = 1 heart lost.
  • Consecutive correct answers build a combo multiplier.
  • Freeze (F) — pauses the timer for 5 s.
  • 50-50 (H) — removes 2 wrong options.
  • Skip (S) — skips without penalty.
  • Infinite mode runs until all 5 hearts are lost.
  • Review all questions on the results screen.

Power-ups
Your Lifelines
F
❄️ Freeze — Pause the Timer
Adds 5 frozen seconds to the clock. Snowflake animation covers the screen while the timer holds. Use on calculation-heavy questions.
H
½ 50-50 — Eliminate Two Wrong Answers
Removes 2 of the 3 wrong options instantly. Converts a 25% guess into a 50% chance. Best used when you've narrowed it down but aren't sure.
S
↷ Skip — Move On Without Penalty
Skips the current question without losing a heart or combo. The question is still logged as skipped in the review screen. Your emergency exit.

Score Formula
How Your Score is Computed
Accuracy × Speed × Combo × Tier
🎯
Accuracy
Base points per correct answer. Wrong answers lose partial credit.
⚡
Speed Bonus
Answer faster than the midpoint of the timer to earn extra speed points.
🔥
Combo Multiplier
3 correct in a row = ×2 · 6 in a row = ×3 · keeps stacking higher.
📈
Tier Multiplier
T1 = 1× · T2 = 1.5× · T3 = 2× — harder tiers are worth much more.
Getting Started
Beginner's Playbook
01
Start on Easy, always
Even if you're experienced, Easy mode calibrates your baseline and warms up your mental arithmetic — don't skip it.
02
Read the SVG explanation
Every wrong answer shows a visual step-by-step breakdown. Don't just retry — understand the diagram first.
03
Save power-ups for T3
Tier 3 questions are substantially harder. Hoard your Freeze and 50-50 tokens for the late-game crunch.
04
Protect your streak above all
A ×3 combo running through T3 is worth more than three separate correct answers. Skip rather than guess wrong.
05
Check the category heatmap
The results screen shows which topic categories you're weakest in. Focus your next session there specifically.
06
Use Infinite to stress-test
Infinite mode's ever-escalating difficulty is the best way to find your real ceiling — play it after every 5 standard sessions.
